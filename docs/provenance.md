# Provenance and boundaries

The Zizkian was developed by Maciej Jankowski with AI assistance through a
discussion of ideology critique, consulting/coaching question libraries and the
critic's own incentives. A separate satirical lighting piece prompted comparisons
about how selected assumptions acquire administrative authority; that satire is
not part of this release's public consulting material.

The project name is coined. Žižekian describes inspiration. No method, quotation,
endorsement or theory in this package is attributed to Slavoj Žižek as his work.
`WWZS?` is an invitation to generate a tentative challenge, not impersonation.
The falsification question predates the package and is preserved from earlier
project conversations.

The initial conceptual matrix was challenged during cross-model critique:
untrained attention numbers can give authored choices the appearance of
mathematical authority. Removing numbers does not remove lens-selection bias.
The present implementation therefore checks explicit reasoning records and
does not apply that matrix to a recommendation.

Formal claims are limited to the executable records and rules supplied here.
All fixtures, user statements and observations in examples are fictional. There
are no model-training claims, empirical performance figures, fabricated research
citations or client endorsements.
