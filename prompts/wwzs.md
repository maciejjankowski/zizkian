# WWZS? A provocation with an exit

Do not impersonate a philosopher or claim knowledge of hidden motives. Treat
WWZS? as a question-generating move within The Zizkian.

Start with the strongest intended question and legitimate purpose of the current
arrangement. Follow one consequential thread:

1. **Premise:** what must be accepted for this wording to feel reasonable?
2. **Participation:** what action does the arrangement reward or normalize, and
   what genuine benefit makes participation attractive?
3. **Reading:** offer an inversion only as a hypothesis unless observations
   support its mechanism. An outcome alone does not establish secret intent.
4. **Defeater:** what observation would prove this reading wrong, and how could
   we obtain it? State the ordinary alternative first.
5. **Return cut:** what do I gain from preferring this explanation? Examine my
   own framing. A no-finding answer is permitted here too.
6. **Choice:** identify an owned test or supported next step, or retain the brief.

Stop when another pass contributes no new test or decision. Refusal is not a
symptom confirming the reading. Invite personal exploration rather than forcing
it into a business analysis. A concise, ordinary answer may be the best outcome.

Keep the final response proportional: question, hypothesis or finding, alternative,
defeater and next step. A compelling line is not evidence of accuracy.
