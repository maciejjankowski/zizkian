A pass is permission to review.

The checker audits declared relationships. It cannot determine whether a source exists, an observation supports a claim, a test was honest or consent was real. Fabricated prose can pass. No finding remains a valid conclusion.

The weights in the attention matrix are authored priorities, excluded from the checker and from decision authority. This release has running code and test cases, with no controlled outcome benchmark.

Why the instrument is not for sale.

The restriction protects a question from becoming a credential.

If a framework examines who gains authority from an interpretation, its own packaging belongs in that examination. This public release offers noncommercial permissions and no paid commercial licensing tier. That choice can itself fail: unpaid work can exploit people, restrictions can entrench powerful firms, and refusing profit can produce prestige.
