Why deliberately false evidence?

Both false-evidence records are labelled fiction. One cites an irrelevant observation; the other invents a credible-sounding research report. Both can pass when the references and declarations are well formed. Missing an owner fails regardless of whether the underlying claim is true. These examples demonstrate the boundary between structural validation and evidence verification. They do not measure the framework’s usefulness.

The onboarding satire offers an interpretation, not a finding that tutorials always conceal defective products. Keep the ordinary explanation available: some tasks require legitimate instruction.
